01 · PRISON, COURTS AND LEGAL AID

Strengthening access to legal aid for persons in custody.

The Vidhi Sanjeevani – Prison, Courts and Legal Aid (PCLA) Programme is an initiative of Maharashtra National Law University (MNLU), Nagpur, dedicated to strengthening access to justice and providing meaningful legal and support services to persons in custody. The programme works to ensure that inmates are able to understand their legal rights, access appropriate legal remedies, and receive timely assistance in matters concerning their cases and well-being.

The programme provides individualised legal assistance based on the needs of each inmate. This includes assistance in obtaining legal representation, bail and surety matters, case-status updates, communication with advocates, and understanding court proceedings. The programme also facilitates video conferencing, mulakhat-related assistance, and coordination with families and relevant stakeholders to address practical difficulties faced by inmates during the course of their cases.

A significant component of Vidhi Sanjeevani is legal awareness and counselling. Inmates are provided with information about their legal rights, available remedies, court procedures, bail provisions, and other relevant legal entitlements. Individual and need-based counselling is also provided to help inmates understand and navigate the legal and personal challenges arising from incarceration. Where required, psychosocial support and counselling for inmates and their families is facilitated as part of the programme’s holistic approach.

Currently, Vidhi Sanjeevani provides support to inmates at Amravati Central Prison (Male), Amravati Central Prison (Women), and Morshi Open Prison. Through regular prison visits and court interventions, the programme identifies the concerns of inmates, follows up on their cases, and coordinates with advocates, courts, prison authorities, legal services institutions, and families to facilitate appropriate assistance.

Ultimately, Vidhi Sanjeevani aims to strengthen the delivery of legal aid to persons in custody by addressing both their legal and support needs. Through sustained legal assistance, awareness, counselling, and coordination with relevant stakeholders, the programme seeks to promote informed decision-making, timely access to legal remedies, and greater responsiveness within the prison and legal aid system.
