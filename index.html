ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

Building a stronger, more inclusive justice ecosystem.

Vidhi Sanjeevani is a flagship access to justice initiative of Maharashtra National Law University (MNLU), Nagpur, implemented with the support of the Azim Premji Foundation. Conceived as a long-term institutional initiative, Vidhi Sanjeevani seeks to strengthen the justice delivery system by improving access to quality legal services and promoting a more inclusive, efficient, and responsive legal ecosystem.

The initiative is guided by the belief that meaningful access to justice extends beyond the existence of legal rights and institutions. It requires competent legal professionals, effective legal aid mechanisms, and institutional interventions that address the practical barriers faced by vulnerable and marginalised communities. Vidhi Sanjeevani therefore adopts a holistic approach to strengthening the legal system through capacity-building, legal empowerment, and collaboration with key stakeholders within the justice sector.

As the host and anchor institution, MNLU Nagpur brings together academic excellence, legal research, and practical engagement to design programmes that respond to contemporary challenges in the administration of justice. The initiative works closely with members of the judiciary, practising advocates, legal services institutions, prisons, government agencies, and civil society organisations to create sustainable and evidence-based interventions.

Vidhi Sanjeevani presently operates through two major components. The first is the Continuing Legal Education (CLE) Programme, which focuses on enhancing the knowledge, skills, and professional competence of practising advocates through structured, practice-oriented training. The second component focuses on Prisons, Courts and Legal Aid (PCLA) Programme, aimed at strengthening access to legal assistance, particularly for undertrial prisoners and other vulnerable populations, while supporting institutional efforts to improve the functioning of the justice delivery system.

Through these complementary initiatives, Vidhi Sanjeevani reflects MNLU Nagpur's commitment to advancing constitutional values, promoting the rule of law, and ensuring that access to justice becomes a lived reality for all. By investing in both legal professionals and justice institutions, the initiative seeks to contribute to a stronger, more equitable, and citizen-centric legal system.
