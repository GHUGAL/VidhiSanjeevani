02 · CONTINUING LEGAL EDUCATION

Strengthening the capacity of legal professionals.

The Continuing Legal Education (CLE) Programme is the flagship component of the Vidhi Sanjeevani initiative of Maharashtra National Law University (MNLU), Nagpur, supported by the Azim Premji Foundation. The programme is designed to strengthen the professional capacity of practising advocates through structured, practice-oriented, and contemporary legal education that responds to the evolving needs of the legal profession and the justice delivery system.

The programme recognises that the legal profession is constantly evolving through legislative reforms, judicial pronouncements, technological advancements, and changing client expectations. To effectively navigate these developments, advocates require opportunities for continuous learning beyond formal legal education. The CLE Programme addresses this need by providing a comprehensive platform for lifelong professional development.

A key objective of the programme is to enhance the quality of legal representation available at the district court level by equipping advocates with updated legal knowledge, practical lawyering skills, and a deeper understanding of professional ethics. The curriculum integrates substantive and procedural law with experiential learning in areas such as legal drafting, trial advocacy, client interviewing and counselling, legal communication, negotiation, legal research, professional responsibility, and technology-enabled legal practice.

The programme places particular emphasis on capacity-building for practising advocates from the underserved districts of Nagpur, Wardha, Yavatmal, Gadchiroli, and Amravati.

Ultimately, the CLE Programme aims to foster a culture of lifelong learning, ethical professional practice, and excellence in advocacy.
